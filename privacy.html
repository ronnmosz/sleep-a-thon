Privacy

Sleep-a-Thon is a small independent project based in Amsterdam. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

Information we collect

This website does not currently use registration forms, advertising or analytics. Sleep-a-Thon does not directly collect personal information simply because you visit the website.

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit the site, GitHub may process technical information, including your IP address, for purposes such as security and the operation of its services.

External services

This website contains links to external services, such as Meetup. If you follow one of these links, you leave the Sleep-a-Thon website and the privacy practices of that external service apply.

Cookies

Sleep-a-Thon does not currently use analytics, advertising or other optional tracking cookies.

Contact

If you have a question about privacy or the use of your personal information in connection with Sleep-a-Thon, you can contact us at sleepathon-collective@proton.me .
